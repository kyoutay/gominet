Over the summer months til now, I've experienced depression again, I've thought. I tend to be prone to it, but this one was different. I was some many states away from home, but what difference does that make? During school, I was away practically the same duration that I was over this summer. But, the geographic distance did matter in a few ways leading to depression. These circumstances took me out of my fanciful detached cocoon; the sort from which I had the mind to write all these other gomi entries. And these past few months I was yanked out of it; felt despair, the kind that puts one in fight or flight mode. I'm no longer in fight or flight, I'm doing better, but there are some vestigial effects. All the sudden, the desire for a network came to. Preoccupation with status, wealth, etc. etc. Why was I in fight or flight? For the first time in my life, my future didn't seem so guaranteed; the path was no longer clear and linear. I learned my mother's retirement was not as secure as I'd imagined, AI threatening livelihoods. Including improving bounds for Navier-Stokes, ravaging early PhD research programs, genocide of entry-level roles (along with some combination of job market uncertainty? which is some combination of trump and ai) For future referrers, all the talk and hype at this time were the hot new OpenAI Astra, Jacob Coxon, etc.



"*What the fuck am I doing studying pure math? What's my other major? Shit, should've gone health or robotics... Things dont seem like they'll pan out the way I planned and hoped for...*". It makes biological sense why my mind would become fixate on status and network at this time; when this creature is truly hopeless, it must puff up its chest, attract those seeking to align with the (seemingly) powerful -> build a safety network... this creature no longer thinks it can do it alone, on its sole ability. Then I realize, this is how most everyone is operating. Most, by definition, are average, and know it. Really explains a lot about them.

So it seems I've come to the masquerade as just myself (same with any other gifted and/or autistic individual. I'm just r4t4rded).

Then, in the face of this reality, some deep visceral/primitive conscience debates the competing thoughts: lack of utility in studying pure math (should spend time learning to whip agents into action instead) vs finish the pure math for the status.

"*You're only 3 classes away-But it's so fucking useless, especially now."*

*"But the status!-But where's the status if the agents are commoditizing reasoning?"*

*"Where will the status be?-Where will the actual value be?*"

It'd have been nice to stay in my cocoon reality. All the people who've lived their entire lives from there. It's nice to conjecture and posit the world from there; what are they thinking even higher up? But now I'm down on the ground, it's pretty scary down here.



I think... I'll just try to be happy. I reflect on my years in the cocoon, that depression was existential. It was even fun to ponder. This despair, however, is debilitating... it provokes all the human ugly in the human fight for livelihood. Unhappiness with an uncertain future, I can't do anything but turn to God and lay down my worries (Understand mechanisms for lowering [default mode network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Default_mode_network) activity. Detachment, avoid chasing thoughts, make that decision or decide to make it later).

After all, what else is this but a chance to experience? An interpretation of a bunch of colored lights?

So! I will drop that class. I will drop the major to a minor with only 3 classes leftover. I will do what I feel like and not what these post-cocoon thoughts cry. It is an exercise in letting go. What will happen when I graduate? Don't know, can't know. We will find out then, there's no use in being sad while waiting... can try to be happy while figuring it out. [It could be much worse.](%28https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negative_visualization%29)
